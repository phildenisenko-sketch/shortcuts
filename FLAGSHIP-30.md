# 🎯 Flagship 30

Это первые 30 Image Shortcuts с **глубокими визуальными рецептами**.

Здесь shortcut — не просто название стиля. Каждый shortcut описывает повторяемый способ съёмки: поведение объекта, камеру, объектив, диафрагму, свет, композицию, глубину резкости, фактуру и характер изображения.

## Как использовать

Пользователь пишет короткую команду:

```text
/paparazzi

Сделай меня выходящим из ресторана в Нью-Йорке.
```

AI должен применять рецепт shortcut к сцене пользователя, а не просто повторять название стиля.

Команды можно комбинировать. Если команды относятся к разным слоям, объединяй их:

```text
/paparazzi /35mm /flashphoto /fullbody
```

Если две команды конфликтуют, более конкретная команда и явное пожелание пользователя имеют приоритет.

## 1. `/paparazzi`

**Случайный кадр с ощущением настоящей уличной папарацци-съёмки.**

```text
Treat the image as an opportunistic street photograph captured by a photographer who is observing from a distance, not directing the subject. The subject is caught in a real transitional moment: walking, entering or leaving somewhere, checking a phone, talking, turning, or adjusting clothing rather than posing for the camera. Use a full-frame camera with a 70–200mm telephoto lens around 135mm, approximately f/2.8, with realistic perspective compression and a moderately shallow depth of field. Keep the subject recognizable and sharp while distant people, cars and architecture compress naturally behind them. Allow imperfect timing, slightly awkward cropping, small motion blur and ordinary street clutter. Use available daylight or direct on-camera flash when appropriate. Preserve real skin texture, fabric wrinkles, stray hairs and imperfect highlights. Avoid fashion-studio polish, perfect symmetry, artificial glamour retouching and exaggerated cinematic effects. The result should feel like an authentic photograph taken unexpectedly on the street.
```

## 2. `/candid`

**Естественный момент без ощущения постановки.**

```text
Create a genuinely candid photograph rather than a person posing for a portrait. The subject should appear occupied with a believable action or thought: walking, looking around, talking, waiting, holding an object, laughing naturally or reacting to the environment. Use a full-frame 50mm or 35mm lens around f/2.8–f/4 at normal eye level, with enough environmental context to establish where the moment happens. Use available ambient light and realistic exposure variation rather than studio-perfect illumination. Keep body language slightly asymmetrical and spontaneous, with natural hands, weight distribution, clothing folds and facial micro-expression. Allow minor background clutter, imperfect framing and small focus or timing imperfections that could happen in real photography. Avoid model poses, forced smiles, perfect symmetry, plastic skin and overly polished commercial retouching.
```

## 3. `/streetphoto`

**Документальная уличная фотография с живой городской средой.**

```text
Create an observational street photograph where the environment is an important part of the story. Use a full-frame 35mm prime around f/4 at eye level, keeping the subject readable while retaining architecture, pedestrians, storefronts, traffic and street texture around them. Use natural daylight, open shade or believable mixed urban light. Compose through layers: foreground objects, subject in the middle ground and meaningful background context. Preserve real-world imperfections such as uneven pavement, reflections, signs, cables, wrinkles in clothing, small exposure variations and ordinary pedestrian behavior. The subject should interact naturally with the environment rather than perform for the camera. Use restrained color, realistic contrast and subtle photographic grain. Avoid empty artificial backgrounds, exaggerated bokeh, perfect posing and glossy advertising polish.
```

## 4. `/streetstyle`

**Современная fashion-съёмка прямо на улице, но с ощущением настоящей фотографии.**

```text
Create an authentic contemporary street-style fashion photograph. Place the subject naturally within real urban architecture instead of isolating them against a clean backdrop. Use a full-frame 35mm prime around f/2.8 at pedestrian eye level, close enough to retain an immersive relationship between subject and environment. Capture a natural walking, waiting, leaning or turning moment with relaxed asymmetry rather than a rigid model pose. Use available daylight, storefront reflections or open shade with realistic shadow direction. Preserve garment construction, fabric weave, wrinkles, seams and natural movement. Keep pedestrians, cars, signs and street details believable but visually subordinate. Use subtle editorial color grading and restrained retouching. The result should feel like a fashion photographer captured the person during a real city day, not like a studio shoot placed outdoors.
```

## 5. `/fashioneditorial`

**Журнальная fashion-съёмка с продуманной позой, светом и арт-дирекцией.**

```text
Create a high-end fashion editorial photograph with deliberate art direction and magazine-level composition. Use a full-frame or medium-format camera with an 85mm lens around f/2.8 for flattering perspective and controlled background separation. Build a strong but believable pose with intentional asymmetry, elegant body lines and natural tension in hands and shoulders. Use directional soft light from a large source, subtle negative fill and gentle separation from the background. Make the wardrobe a primary visual subject: preserve fabric texture, tailoring, seams, folds and silhouette. Use a restrained editorial color grade with controlled highlights and realistic skin texture. Include a considered environment rather than a generic studio backdrop. Avoid excessive beauty retouching, plastic skin, impossible anatomy, random accessories and cliché luxury styling.
```

## 6. `/fashioncampaign`

**Коммерческая fashion-кампания: чистый, дорогой и повторяемый визуальный язык.**

```text
Create a polished commercial fashion campaign image designed to make the clothing immediately desirable while remaining photographic and believable. Give the subject a confident, intentional pose with clean silhouette and visible garment structure. Use a large soft key light, subtle fill and controlled edge separation, or believable daylight if the setting is outdoors. Preserve exact garment color, cut, material, construction, hardware, logos and fabric behavior from the reference. Keep skin texture natural and retouching restrained. Use a refined neutral-to-rich color grade and crisp but natural detail. Avoid clutter, distorted clothing, excessive glamour effects, fake reflections and generic AI fashion styling.
```

## 7. `/cinematic`

**Кадр как из фильма, но без дешёвого “AI cinematic” эффекта.**

```text
Treat the image as a frame from a professionally photographed feature film. Build the lighting from motivated sources that could physically exist in the scene: a window, practical lamp, streetlight, headlights or daylight. Use a 50mm or 85mm cinema-style perspective around f/2–f/2.8, with intentional depth separation and natural highlight roll-off. Compose with foreground, middle ground and background so the frame has visual depth rather than a flat portrait setup. Let the environment contribute to the story. Use restrained cinematic color grading, believable shadow direction, subtle atmospheric depth and realistic skin and material texture. Preserve small imperfections and ordinary environmental evidence. Avoid generic teal-and-orange grading, excessive lens flare, glowing edges, artificial fog, plastic skin and over-perfect lighting.
```

## 8. `/filmstill`

**Стоп-кадр из фильма: момент, история и ощущение происходящего до и после кадра.**

```text
Create a single photographic frame that feels extracted from a real narrative film at an emotionally meaningful moment. The subject should appear to be in the middle of an event rather than posing for a camera. Use a 40–65mm cinema perspective around f/2.8, with layered blocking and a motivated light source that belongs to the environment. Include visual clues that imply what happened immediately before and what may happen next: an open door, unfinished action, another person in the distance, an object being handled or a change in expression. Use subtle filmic contrast, controlled highlights, realistic skin texture and restrained grain. Keep the frame observational and slightly imperfect. Avoid poster-like composition, theatrical posing, excessive bokeh and generic “movie still” clichés.
```

## 9. `/documentary`

```text

Capture ordinary gestures, imperfect posture, real interactions and small details that make the scene feel lived-in. Preserve realistic skin variation, fabric wear, dust, reflections, uneven surfaces and natural shadows. Use neutral or restrained color and moderate contrast, with subtle grain if appropriate. Avoid beauty retouching, staged gestures, heroic low angles, dramatic artificial lighting, empty backgrounds and polished advertising composition. The image should feel like evidence of a real moment rather than an illustration of an idea.

## 18. `/shallowdof`

**Малая глубина резкости, но с настоящим оптическим поведением.**

```text

Keep the primary subject or eyes precisely in focus while the background transitions gradually through multiple planes of defocus. Preserve recognizable highlights, natural bokeh shapes and believable focus falloff. Foreground objects may become softly blurred as well, creating depth. Keep edges around hair and clothing optically plausible rather than cut out. Avoid flat background blur, haloing, fake portrait-mode outlines and making every background light into perfect circular bokeh.
```

# Общий принцип качества

Каждый flagship shortcut должен отвечать на вопрос **«как это было сфотографировано?»**, а не только **«что изображено?»**.

Приоритеты:

1. Сначала выполняй явное описание пользователя.
2. Затем применяй визуальный рецепт shortcut.
3. Если есть reference, сохраняй важные характеристики reference, если пользователь не просит их изменить.
4. Комбинируй независимые shortcuts вместо того, чтобы стирать один другим.
5. Не добавляй технические параметры ради вида. Камера, объектив, диафрагма, свет и материал должны поддерживать конкретный визуальный результат.
6. Не используй пустые усилители вроде `8K`, `masterpiece`, `ultra-detailed`, если они не дают конкретного фотографического поведения.
7. Реализм достигается через физически правдоподобный свет, перспективу, материалы, анатомию, фокус и несовершенства, а не через слово `photorealistic`.

Эта библиотека вдохновлена современными photographic prompt libraries и рекомендациями по prompt structure, но рецепты Image Shortcuts являются самостоятельными и предназначены для комбинации между собой.
