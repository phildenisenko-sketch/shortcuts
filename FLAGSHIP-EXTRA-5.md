# ⭐ Flagship Extra 5

These five shortcuts are part of the practical flagship set because they appeared in the project's real-world examples and cover important use cases beyond the original Flagship 30.

Together with `FLAGSHIP-30.md`, this makes a **Flagship 35** set.

## 31. `/action`

**Динамичная спортивная или lifestyle-съёмка с физически правдоподобным движением.**

```text
Create a dynamic action photograph that looks captured at the decisive moment by a real sports or lifestyle photographer. The subject must perform a physically believable action with correct weight distribution, joint angles, balance, clothing movement and contact with the environment. Use a full-frame camera with a 70–200mm lens around 135mm for compressed action framing, or 35–50mm when environmental context is important. Use a fast shutter around 1/1000–1/2000s for a crisp decisive moment, unless intentional motion blur is explicitly requested. Track the subject naturally so the main face or body remains sharp while secondary movement may blur. Use available daylight or believable event lighting, realistic shadows and restrained color grading. Preserve fabric tension, hair movement, dust, water or other physical evidence of motion. Avoid frozen mannequin poses, impossible body mechanics, floating limbs, excessive speed lines and videogame-like effects.
```

## 32. `/underwearcampaign`

**Коммерческая рекламная съёмка нижнего белья: продукт, посадка, материал и бренд в центре кадра.**

```text
Create a premium commercial underwear campaign photograph for an adult fashion brand. Treat the underwear as the primary product: preserve its exact cut, color, material, seams, waistband, stitching, hardware, logos and fit from the reference. Use an adult model and a professional fashion-photography setup with deliberate but non-sexual posing that clearly shows the garment's construction and silhouette. Use a full-frame 50–85mm lens around f/2.8–f/4, with soft directional key light, controlled fill and clean separation from the background. Show realistic fabric tension, folds, elasticity, skin texture and natural body proportions. Use polished but believable commercial color grading and restrained retouching. The environment should support the brand rather than distract from the product. Avoid erotic staging, exaggerated body proportions, artificial skin, distorted garments, invented product details and generic AI fashion styling.
```

## 33. `/instagram`

**Instagram-ready visual with strong mobile-first composition and immediate subject readability.**

```text
Create a polished social-media photograph designed to read immediately on a smartphone screen. Prioritize one clear visual subject, strong silhouette, clean separation and a composition that remains legible when viewed as a small thumbnail. Use a contemporary editorial or lifestyle photographic treatment appropriate to the user's subject rather than a generic social-media filter. Keep facial features, products, clothing and important details crisp enough to survive mobile reduction. Use natural contrast, controlled highlights and a restrained color grade with enough tonal separation to feel vivid without looking artificially processed. Leave intentional breathing room when text or interface overlays may be added later. Avoid cluttered backgrounds, tiny subjects, excessive HDR, fake bokeh and generic influencer aesthetics.
```

## 34. `/carousel`

**Instagram carousel slide system: consistent visual language across a 1080×1350 vertical series.**

```text
Create a vertical Instagram carousel slide in a 1080×1350, 4:5 composition. Treat this as one frame in a coherent multi-slide visual system, not as an isolated poster. Establish a clear hierarchy with one dominant subject or message area, strong margins and enough negative space for optional typography. Keep important faces, products and objects away from the extreme edges so platform cropping and UI overlays do not interfere. Use consistent camera perspective, lighting direction, color treatment, texture and visual density so adjacent slides can belong to the same series. If the user provides a reference slide, preserve its visual language while changing only the requested content. Avoid random composition changes, overcrowding, tiny unreadable elements and decorative details that compete with the main message.
```

## 35. `/lingeriecampaign`

**Коммерческая fashion/editorial кампания для бренда женского белья с акцентом на продукт и единый брендовый визуальный язык.**

```text
Create a premium adult lingerie campaign image for a fashion brand. Treat the lingerie as a commercial product and preserve the reference garment's exact design, color, lace, mesh, stitching, straps, hardware, construction and fit. Use an adult model with natural anatomy and a confident fashion pose focused on showing the garment clearly. Use a full-frame 50–85mm lens around f/2.8–f/4, controlled soft directional lighting and subtle separation from the environment. Preserve realistic skin texture, fabric transparency and material response without inventing details. Use a sophisticated editorial color grade and restrained professional retouching. Keep the scene clearly within fashion advertising, catalog or editorial photography. Avoid erotic staging, explicit sexual framing, exaggerated anatomy, plastic skin and generic AI glamour effects.
```
